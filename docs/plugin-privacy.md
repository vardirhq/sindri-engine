# Sindri Game Dev privacy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This notice describes **Vardir's Sindri Game Dev plugin**, a package of
instructions and reference documents. It does not describe every Sindri
Engine application or every page on this website.

## Data handled by the plugin

The published package contains no Vardir-operated server connection, telemetry,
analytics, account system or payment integration. It does not itself send chat
messages, uploaded files or project contents to Vardir, and it has no separate
Vardir chat-data store or retention schedule.

Your host platform processes conversations and files supplied to the assistant
under its own terms, privacy policy and account settings. Any repository,
terminal, browser or other tool you enable may process additional information
under that tool provider's policies. The plugin does not override those settings
or give Vardir access to your private repositories.

## When you contact support

If you email chris@vardir.no, Vardir receives your email address and the content
you send to respond to your request. Send only the information needed for the
request. For questions about support correspondence, access or deletion,
contact that address; applicable rights and any necessary retention depend on
the circumstances and applicable law.

GitHub issues are public and handled through GitHub. Your account details,
issue text and attachments may be visible to others. Do not post confidential
information. GitHub's own privacy policy also applies.

## Website visits and third parties

Opening this notice or linked websites makes a separate request to their hosting
providers. Hosting providers may process connection information such as IP
addresses for delivery and security. Website processing is separate from the
plugin package; this notice does not claim that the host platform, GitHub or
linked websites collect no data.

## Changes and contact

This notice will be updated if the plugin's data handling changes. Contact
[chris@vardir.no](mailto:chris@vardir.no) with privacy questions.
